At some point a founder has to transition from founder to executive if their company is to grow or they are to keep their job. Promoting, building, and marketing a new production or solution is great, but at some point other people will do that for you. You must be able to lead these people, build confidence in the team, and provide proper direction. You have to be a leader. Why the hell would anybody trust news from social networking? I understand that something like 40% of young adults do, which is an indication that some people trust anything that is socially validated.
