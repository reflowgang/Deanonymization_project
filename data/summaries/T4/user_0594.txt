Just wanted to point out a few differences to people hitting PGSQL after getting their feet wet with MySQL. 1. While installing PGSQL get your configuration variables correct, or it wont work out-of-the-box (kernel.shmmax, autovacuum, shared_buffers) 2. learn about authentication. PGSQL depends on authentication of the OS ("sameuser" on pg_hba.conf). MySQL behaves by default as the "password" authentication method of PGSQL. 3. Learn about template databases. Your first database is a template database. 4. PGSQL has "pg_" databases and slash commands (\dt, \connect, \q) for system administration. Sorry - I should have clarified.
