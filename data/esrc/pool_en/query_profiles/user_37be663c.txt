First of all, even though Nexus Mod Manager is easier to learn, [Mod Organizer](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/1334/?) is a better alternative. It installs mods in a separated folder from game files, so you will have less issues if your mod setup doesn't work. Something really important: use [LOOT](https://loot.github.io/) to sort your mod load order after having installed a few of them. To start, install [SKSE](http://skse.silverlock.org/), it's required for most mods now. Then, [SkyUI](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/3863/?) should be your first mod, it's a requirement for a ton of other mods and has a better mouse/keyboard UI. If you want to keep the classic menus (to play with a controller for instance), install [SkyUI Away](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/29440/?). Now, if you've got the dlcs, install [Unofficial Skyrim Legendary patch](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/71214/?). It's a mod that has a lot of fixes to glitches and problems that were not fixed by Bethesda. If you only have the vanilla game, or not all the dlcs, install the other versions ([vanilla](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/19/?), [Dragonborn](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/31083/?), [Dawnguard](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/23491/?), [Heartfire](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/25127/?)). If you're playing with mouse and keyboard, install [Better Dialogue Controls](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/27371/?) and [Better MessageBox Controls](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/28170/?) to make your life easier navigating the menus and selecting options. Now, some mods to improve the game overall: - [Immersive HUD - iHUD](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/3222/?) allows to show or hide and customize the HUD; - [Enhanced Camera](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/57859/?): see your character's body in first person; - [VioLens - A Killmove Mod](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/56980/?): customize your killmoves; - [Customizable Camera](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/37347/?): customize your camera options; - [No Spinning Death Animation](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/17214/?): removes the stupid little dance npcs make when dying; - [Main Menu](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/64948/?): see more info about the save you're loading; - [Extensible Follower Framework](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/12933/?): there are other follower mods but this one is great, allows you to have ahuge range of options for them; - [Dynamic Follower Dialogue - DFD](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/69541/?): adds a lot more dialogue to followers; - [Timing is Everything](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/38151/?): gives you control over a lot of quests so that you can delay them or start them earlier; - [Run For Your Lives](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/23906/?): non-fighters npcs will stop playing heroes when attacked by dragons and flee instead, [When Vampires Attack](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/28235/?) does the same thing for vampire attacks. Some other mods I like: - [Simply Knock](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/73236/?): you can knock on npcs' doors and they can let you in (if they want to), which is super useful sometimes; - [Quick Loot](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/73096/?): allows you to loot stuff from containers/corpses or whatever without opening the menu, has a reskin with better UI as well; - [Combat Evolved](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/56147/?): a great mod to enhance enemy AI that works both with vanilla and combat mods, one of my favorites; - [All Geared Up](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/40953/?): you can see all the weapons you have equipped on your character; - [Skyrim Unbound](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/71465/?): an alternate start mod that lets you configure tons of options (like being dragonborn or not) and your starting location, or even make everything random. Anyway, these are some great mods, not all essentials, but they should improve on the vanilla game.

The combat in Dks2 is deep. Yes, it is way slower than Dks3 and even slower than Dks1. The movement also feels more floaty and the weapon impacts have no real weight. However, the level of control on your actions is still here. There are a lot of weapons, some of them have the same moves and different looks/range/stats only. That said, there is a lot of weapon variety still, and you have tons of options depending on your build. Powerstance is also a cool feature, which allows you to use different weapon combinations to use dual attacks with L1 and L2 (you'll need high stats though). That said, the game is awesome. It has tons of content, a lot of variety in levels and bosses (though level design is a step back from the other games), lots of weapons, viable magic/sorceries/pyro/faith (though faith is mostly for buffs in this game). There are a lot of bosses (31 in vanilla, 40 with the dlcs), most of the vanilla ones are forgettable though (most have cool concepts I think). The dlcs and their bosses are meant for end-game, way harder than vanilla, and are among the best content in all Souls-games in my opinion, with awesome bossfights. Overall, if you can get over worse level design/world design, less memorable vanilla bosses, and a different and slightly slower gameplay, then you'll enjoy it a lot, it has tons of replayability (and a great pvp as well). Be sure to play Sotfs on Ps4, Xbox One or PC to get the best experience. I'll copy-paste an answer to a similar question: First of all, even though Nexus Mod Manager is easier to learn, [Mod Organizer](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/1334/?) is a better alternative. It installs mods in a separated folder from game files, so you will have less issues if your mod setup doesn't work. That said, you can start modding with NMM if you want at first, just think about switching over to MO if you start installing lots of stuff. Something really important: use [LOOT](https://loot.github.io/) to sort your mod load order after having installed a few of them. To start, install [SKSE](http://skse.silverlock.org/), it's required for most mods now. Then, [SkyUI](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/3863/?) should be your first mod, it's a requirement for a ton of other mods and has a better mouse/keyboard UI. If you want to keep the classic menus (to play with a controller for instance), install [SkyUI Away](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/29440/?). Now, if you've got the dlcs, install [Unofficial Skyrim Legendary patch](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/71214/?). It's a mod that has a lot of fixes to glitches and problems that were not fixed by Bethesda. If you only have the vanilla game, or not all the dlcs, install the other versions ([vanilla](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/19/?), [Dragonborn](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/31083/?), [Dawnguard](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/23491/?), [Heartfire](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/25127/?)). If you're playing with mouse and keyboard, install [Better Dialogue Controls](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/27371/?) and [Better MessageBox Controls](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/28170/?) to make your life easier navigating the menus and selecting options. Now, some mods to improve the game overall: - [Immersive HUD - iHUD](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/3222/?) allows to show or hide and customize the HUD; - [Enhanced Camera](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/57859/?): see your character's body in first person; - [VioLens - A Killmove Mod](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/56980/?): customize your killmoves; - [Customizable Camera](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/37347/?): customize your camera options; - [No Spinning Death Animation](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/17214/?): removes the stupid little dance npcs make when dying; - [Main Menu](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/64948/?): see more info about the save you're loading; - [Extensible Follower Framework](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/12933/?): there are other follower mods but this one is great, allows you to have ahuge range of options for them; - [Dynamic Follower Dialogue - DFD](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/69541/?): adds a lot more dialogue to followers; - [Timing is Everything](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/38151/?): gives you control over a lot of quests so that you can delay them or start them earlier; - [Run For Your Lives](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/23906/?): non-fighters npcs will stop playing heroes when attacked by dragons and flee instead, [When Vampires Attack](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/28235/?) does the same thing for vampire attacks. Some other mods I like: - [Simply Knock](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/73236/?): you can knock on npcs' doors and they can let you in (if they want to), which is super useful sometimes; - [Quick Loot](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/73096/?): allows you to loot stuff from containers/corpses or whatever without opening the menu, has a reskin with better UI as well; - [Combat Evolved](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/56147/?): a great mod to enhance enemy AI that works both with vanilla and combat mods, one of my favorites; - [All Geared Up](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/40953/?): you can see all the weapons you have equipped on your character; - [Skyrim Unbound](http://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/71465/?): an alternate start mod that lets you configure tons of options (like being dragonborn or not) and your starting location, or even make everything random. Anyway, these are some great mods, not all essentials, but they should improve on the vanilla game. Most of them shouldn't impact your performances that much.
