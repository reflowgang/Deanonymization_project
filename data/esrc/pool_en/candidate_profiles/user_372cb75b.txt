Anything attached to it (Auras, Equipment, Fortifications) all *also* phase out along with it, and they all phase back in whenever the thing they were attached to (but don't phase back in on their own). It's called "indirect phasing". If an attached thing phases out *directly*; that is, it *itself* phases out, rather than the thing it was attached to phasing out, then when it phases back in, it won't be attached to anything. For an Aura that's usually a quick trip to the GY. You won't get to chose something else for the Aura to enchant.

121 possible different packs, but NOT 121 different themes (46 different themes total). There are 3 different rarity of themes, Common, Rare, and Mythic. "Common" themes (eg Dinosaurs, WIzards, Vampires) have 4 different possible variations, Rare themes (eg Cats, Dragons, Dogs) have 2 possible variations, and Mythic themes (eg Liliana, Mill, Raindbow) have only 1 variation. The list of themes and their rarities is here: [https://mtg.gamepedia.com/Jumpstart](https://mtg.gamepedia.com/Jumpstart)
