It's going to depend on how much you like star wars mainly. The game is imo fun, the combat takes some time to get used to (it's not hack and slash, it's careful use of each button, attack animations can't be cancelled to block or dodge and there's a heavy focus on parrying to create openings). Story is well done (though I feel like they played it safe in that regard). The levels are fairly big and as you play you unlock more abilities which will let you access new parts of planets you've already finished. Now for the bad. The game feels short, you will basically be at the end of the game by time you've unlocked all the skills. You spend the majority of the game with only blue or green lightsaber options, you don't unlock more colours until nearly the end of the game (especially if you've been doing all the exploration and chest hunting as you go). Bugs, I personally didn't see any game breaking bugs but there are a lot of minor ones that become more and more noticeable the more you play it. Character customisation is hit or miss, you get about 5 colour swaps of the base outfit and about 15-20 ponchos that you can choose to wear but those are all just colour swaps too with some minor variations in the shape. The only reason to replay is if you want to try a higher difficulty level, there's no new game+ so you will be starting from scratch and there aren't any achievements for beating the game at higher difficulty levels. There's also no fast travel so there's a lot of walking about, especially when you've finished a planet and have to go back to your ship. Personally I've got my money's worth out of it, I'm on my 4th playthrough (2nd one at highest difficulty after playthroughs 1-3 I increased difficulty each time). Buy if you're more of a one and done type then I don't think it's worth full price. It's a fun game while it lasts and I loved it but you will almost certainly be left wanting more by the end of it and you can't replay boss fights apart from starting over so by the end all that's left is killing regular enemies and you have to retread the maps to do that again (an arena mode would have been great). However if you feel like you might want to replay and get good enough for the higher difficulties then you're more likely to feel like you got your money's worth

Continue story mainly, there are a lot of quests between Ultima weapon and heavenward and most of them aren't very interesting but it has to be done to continue. The only beast tribe I'd suggest at the moment is the ixal, they are the crafter tribe of arr so you can slowly level a crafter using their quests (you will be given all you need to do the craft during the quest and you can use any craft job), the rest just offer regular so for combat jobs, you will unlock things like mounts and certain dyes that aren't available at the regular vendor but imo they aren't worth focusing on just yet. Poetics use for ironworks gear in mor dhona for now, it's the basically the best lvl 50 gear (aside from relic weapon and maybe some raid gear but it's not worth working that hard on something that will only be a glamour pretty soon). After that save them because they can be used on lvl 60 and 70 gear too when you get to the required part of those expansions. I definitely recommend unlocking gold saucer, it's a fun side activity to do when you're bored or waiting for a queue to pop, check out the challenge log and you'll see you can get thousands of mgp (gold saucer currency) every week for completing the gold saucer challenges, stuff like play 10 matches of triple triad, participate in 5 gates (gates are basically gold saucer fates), successfully complete 3 gates etc. Imo it's best to just keep chugging away at it, there are mounts that cost millions of mgp which is not fun to try and grind in one go but the challenge log will keep a steady stream of mgp coming in. Also the fashion report there gives 20k just for taking part and an additional 60k if you get over 80 points (there is a very helpful person who posts a guide here weekly, at your level you might not be able to get the 80 points every week depending on what the required items are but even 20k is still nice).
