Once a software system gets past the hello world stage of complexity, its number of components increase. Each interaction between each component is a potential source of bugs. The number of interactions increases by a factorial of the number of components. When the software is running, events are fired off and executed. Each sequence of events is also a potential source of bugs. The number of sequence of events also increases by a factorial of the number of events. See, not all bugs come from negligence and laziness. A perfect software system is a mathematical impossibility.
