The experience of failure is pretty important. Trying something once and failing is okay. Try it again, learn from your mistake. If you fail again, chances are if it's something you can do the third time is much more likely to be successful. You've done it a few times, learned what not to do and thought a lot more about how to do it. You've probably develop at least some muscle memory and if you're already failed a couple times, you've got that fear out of the way. A lot of doing something is getting over that initial fear of failure. It's like trying to ride a skateboard on a halfpipe. You are going to fall the first time you try it, so it's easy to talk yourself out of doing it. If you just suck it up, do it, take the fall, you get over that fear and can then move onto overcoming any true physical rather than psychological barrier.
