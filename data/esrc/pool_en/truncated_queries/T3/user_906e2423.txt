Do you mean connect or do you mean pair? Generally they prevent you from pairing while the car is moving as they fear you are driving and aren't paying attention to the road while doing the pairing. If you mean actually connecting a paired device, probably what you are seeing is that the car stopped trying to connect to the phone after a bit. So if you get in the car, start driving, and then turn on bluetooth on your phone, the car has already given up. Sometimes they will periodically check for a connection say every five minutes or so. In essence the car sends out a broadcast saying I am here and ready for you to connect to. The phone sees that broadcast and says I am paired with that guy, I should connect. If the car has given up it stops broadcasting it is there so the phone doesn't know to try and connect.
