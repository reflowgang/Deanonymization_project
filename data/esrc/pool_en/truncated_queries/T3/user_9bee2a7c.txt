Want to know the crazy thing with old F1 cars? You steer with the wheel, throttle, and brake. All 3. It makes sense once you get it, but until then it's maddening.
If you go into the corner hot and brake late, the car gets unsettled. If you go in slow and leave hot, the car gets unsettled. If you go in at the right speed, you probably have significant understeer. So your only option is one of the first two, using the gas and/or brake to control your turn. Very often you find yourself using all three.
