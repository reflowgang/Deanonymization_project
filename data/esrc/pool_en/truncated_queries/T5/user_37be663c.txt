About the multiplayer, it doesn't use your soul level but your soul memory, which you can see in the stats screen. So you have really no reason to stop leveling. About the Pursuer, I had to fight him around 15 times my first playthrough. Leveling your adaptability helps a lot, and to get souls and to learn how to fight him you could drop a small white soapstone just before the ladder of the platform where you encounter him in the first place.
You shouldn't use an effigy every time you die, or you'll run out pretty quick. A bit later in the game you wil find a ring that will prevent going below 75% of your max health when in hollow form. Right now, what you can do is get the key to the Majula Mansion in the forest of giants from Cale, then in the Mansion you'll find a Pharros Lockstone. If you use it in the area below the Ballista traps near Pate, then you can get the Chloranthy ring, which regens your stamina faster. If you can, try leveling your adaptability around 10 to get more invincibility frames on your rolls. Always try to keep some space from the boss during the battle so that you can roll backwards if low on health. Always roll in the opposite directions the attacks are coming, it makes them easier to dodge (except the grap attack where his sword changes color, just avoid it). If you need to heal, try do do it by dodging his attack and walking backwards using a lifegem, the best moment to heal being when you dodge the last hit of his three hit combo. In my opinion his most dangerous attack is when he does his three hit combo but with the first hit sped up, it's really hard to dodge. Overall it's a pretty hard boss, but the good news is that if you manage to kill him alone you'll probably get a lot better after that. And don't worry about having problems in the game at first, everyboy has difficulties at first (took me around 15-20 tries to kill the pursuer the first time I played). And if you still can't kill him, drop your summon sign, get summoned, help kill him either at the first platform where you encounter him or in his boss arena, and then you'll be human again and will be able to summon people to help you for the fight.
