The elements of the grounds are extracted roughly in this order: * Caffeine * Flavor/Aromatic oils * Bittering agents The first brew is going to get the caffeine and flavor oils and some of the bittering agents. Any subsequent brews are just going to extract more bittering agents, leaving you with flavorless, bitter coffee with very little caffeine content. You can see this by making "cowboy coffee". Set a pot of water to boil. Dump in some coffee grounds, and taste the coffee after 5 seconds, 30 seconds, 1 minute, 5 minutes, 10 minutes. Your first taste will taste like dirty water. Later tastes will taste like how you think of as coffee - oily mouthfeel with some bitterness. The longer you let the coffee sit there boiling, the more it'll just taste of bitterness.
