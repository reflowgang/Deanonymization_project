As for complaining about the LOTR vibe, that's a bit of a EotW-ism. The Great Hunt shifts tone pretty significantly, The Dragon Reborn moreso, and from there WoT has it's own voice for the rest of the series. All of the characters are going to grow and develop, some in completely unexpected ways. Everyone you like will reach a growth point that feels totally satisfying, and some of your "meh" characters may end up surprising you. Welcome to the Wheel. I'm glad you're enjoying it! I envy people going through it for the first time. I read Eye of the World in about a week, got hooked, and then read books 2-7 in about a week's time. Fastest I've ever binged on books in my life. I think I only slept 2-3 hours each night while I read through all those books. It was intense and insane and wonderful.
EotW is tonally and structurally different. There's going to be a few continuity hitches, nothing major, as you progress compared to EotW. Think of it like a really good series. The pilot and first couple episodes are feeling out where they need to be as a show and a story and the tone will vary and the story structure will shuffle around a little looking for the sweet spot. EotW is the pilot, TGH is the first few episodes (although really the changes feel like they were mostly settled on in TGH and just get polished moving foward), and by TDR you've hit the sweet spot and just go.
