Instead, you'll pay 12% during the whole duration of the pay-off process.

There are currently plans for like $20-$50/month that probably will give you the same amount of data and minutes that you are using now. You're probably on a contract now, but when you're ready to switch, research your options. That would be a savings of multiple hundreds of dollars per year. As for your student loans: it might be beneficial to pay them off quicker. You have more income than expenses right now, your credit card debt will be gone once you have that tax refund, maybe it's time to start focusing on the student loans. In particular, if there are loans in there that have an interest rate over 5%. You can check out http://unbury.us to create a payoff plan, that page allows you to play with the numbers ("What if I pay an extra $100/month?") and then you can see how much quicker you can pay off your loans and how much interest that saves you (it tells you how much interest you'll pay, and you can of course compare the two numbers). Your father's situation is different. Ultimately, that's not your problem, and also a thing that you can't fix, really, because *he* has to fix it. I'm not sure whether you would want to stick around for a bit longer to help him, but maybe for the benefit of your sister? But yes, that's a different discussion. Most important thing for you is to get and keep your own finances in good shape. I think budgeting, trying to get better deals next time on expenses like car (transport) and phone, and getting rid of debts are smart things to do. Good luck!
