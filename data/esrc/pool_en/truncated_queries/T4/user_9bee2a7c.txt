If you go in at the right speed, you probably have significant understeer. So your only option is one of the first two, using the gas and/or brake to control your turn. I literally have no idea why both engines both shut down at exactly the same time. I configure it to glide, get the APU turned on (it starts, good sign). Still don't panic, work towards air starting the engines. Still don't panic, engine one starts and goes, engine two starts and goes. Don't panic, just flew over the Tennessee-Georgia border, and I know up ahead is McGee-Tyson, so I can put it down very safely there. Still not panicking, pull up the charts for approach and accept a vectored arrival for emergency. Praying the whole time, I nail the approach on the dots. Engines didn't shut down again.
