/r/GetMotivated likes to encourage an animal ferocity that pretends that luck doesn't exist. They're wrong. The worst is when it's someone like Peter Dinklage acting like his work ethic somehow discounts the role of luck or that acknowledging that luck plays a role in life undercuts his effort. It doesn't on either count. No one is ever saying that the successful weren't hard-working or that they weren't exceptional or that their hard work mitigated the role of luck, but they still got lucky in a myriad of ways that no one can plan nor account for. They weren't randomly struck by lightning or delayed that extra 5 minutes by a traffic accident that prevented them from a crucial meeting they might never be aware was crucial, and they were lucky to be born into an economic class with reasonable health that allowed them to pursue the opportunities they wanted in the first place. Acknowledging these factors does not take away from someone being exceptional, it just acknowledges that they're not gods. It takes the truly brave to understand this and go at something whole-hearted anyway.
