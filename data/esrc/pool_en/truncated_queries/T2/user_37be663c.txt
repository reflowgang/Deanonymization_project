Stats like STR and DEX hit a cap at 40, 90 is really overkill. But basically for ripostes you want to use an ultra greatsword to get heavy dmg on ripostes and guardbreak.
With low vit the best could be either menu-swapping to an UGS when you parry /guardbreak someone, though you need training to do it consistently, or using a dagger. Daggers have lower riposte/guardbreak dmg, but they have a fast backstab startup animation (the best backstab dagger at 99 dex being the Umbral Dagger, and the best for most critical attacks being the Shadow Dagger). You won't get as much dmg as with an Ultra weapon though of course.
