I too have been using this mod heavily the past month or so and it really changes the game in interesting ways. For instance, as long as you stay away from Helgen and don't choose one of the alternate lives which start the main quest, then there is no main quest -- no dragon born, no dragons, no Miraak cultists from Solstheim trying to kill you for no good reason (dragon born envy, wtf!) The most interesting of the half-dozen alternate lives I've tried was the "Left for Dead" life which had my character wake up on the far side of Solstheim over by the Dwarven ruins, with nothing but miner clothes and boots. At least she had boots. No weapons. With some careful movement and some swimming, I made it to Raven Rock, went to where the guard captain fights the ash spawn, looted the gear off the dead guard, helped kill off the remaining ash spawn and thus had the best gear of any start yet: Full bone mold gear with elven sword and bow/arrows. This mod is really valuable and well done. When used in conjunction with Familiar Faces (sorry, can't link to nexusmods, they are down or something) it is even better yet. When used with a follower mod like AFT or EFF, it makes previous characters of yours available as followers. I didn't think too much about this until I actually tried it. All my frustrations (well, most of them -- voice acting limitations are really annoying) with followers made by other people went away when I could use my own previous characters as followers.
