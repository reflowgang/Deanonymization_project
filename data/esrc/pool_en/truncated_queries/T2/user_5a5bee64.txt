The real reason is that males benefit more from variability than females do from an evolutionary standpoint. Females can only produce so many offspring, but a very successful male can produce far more offspring by mating with large numbers of females. Thus, there is an evolutionary advantage to males being more variable, because a highly successful male is going to have, say, 50 more offspring, while a highly successful female is going to have, say, 2 more offspring. Thus, males are rewarded more for being successful than females - but also punished more for being failures, as a male can easily produce zero offspring, while most females will produce at least some baseline amount.

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dunning%E2%80%93Kruger_effect 1,000/292,000,000 is EXACTLY THE SAME as 1/292,000. Use a calculator if you don't believe me; both come out to the exact same number (~3.4 x 10^-6). This is basic elementary school math here. Do you not think that 9/27 is the same as 1/3? If you have a common number in the numerator and the denominator, you can divide both the numerator and the denominator by that number and get the exact same fraction. Another way of thinking about it: If every one of 292 million people bought a lottery ticket with a different number on it, then there would be a 100% chance of someone winning, with each person having a 1 in 292,000,000 chance of winning. If 292,000 people bought 1,000 lottery tickets each, with no duplicate numbers, that would be 292 million lottery tickets purchased - the same as in the above case - and again, there would be a 100% chance of someone winning. Ergo, the odds *must be* 1,000 times greater. Obviously in real life, people buy lots of duplicate lottery numbers, so even if 292,000,000 tickets are bought, you don't always get a winner, but if you're buying all the tickets, then you won't get any overlap because you know what numbers you picked.
