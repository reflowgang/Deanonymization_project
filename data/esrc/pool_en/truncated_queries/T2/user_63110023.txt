I think everyone could benefit from the Christian model of a good community. Admittedly, there are many churches that don't set a very good example. But I'm talking about the ones that do it right. Growing up in the church has been beneficial to me in countless ways, but one really big one has been realizing the power of a community in which people love and support one another despite their vastly different backgrounds, circumstances, and perspectives. It's incredible to see how a group of people, who would otherwise probably never be in the same room together, can be drawn together by their common understanding that happiness comes from serving others and their common goal of pursuing that which they believe to be good and pure.
