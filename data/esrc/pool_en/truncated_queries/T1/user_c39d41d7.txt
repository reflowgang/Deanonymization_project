No, it does, and he's right. The question is who deserves it, the people who have money, or the people who do work? A pure capitalist society doesn't work. Besides just the tragedy of the commons, capitalism tends to coalesce wealth all at the top, leading to things like [robber barons](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robber_baron_(industrialist\)). Capitalism does a ton of things extremely well, but it's a system designed by man and not by god. We need to address where it fails, which is not terribly difficult to do. We did a fairly decent job of it between WW2 and Reagan. We had a healthy middle class, where one family member was able to own a house and provide for his family. Education wasn't terribly expensive, and there was opportunity for everyone to reach at least that healthy middle class somewhat easily. We *landed on the moon* during this time. We can do this again.
Manipulating the system that determines how much value you provide to society is much easier than actually providing value to society.
