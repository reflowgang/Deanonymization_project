Eye contact is not just "eye contact" though, and some people don't realize this. Often you can tell when someone is making eye contact just to make eye contact, and often they have these unnatural, big wide-open eyes that are clearly strained. Or, while you are "trying" to make eye contact, they make eye contact with you, and then you shift your mannerism somehow, or your eyes get larger, or whatever, even in the most subtle way, to their eyes, makes it come off as creepy. Eye contact is important, but it needs to be natural, and this is a hard challenge for a lot of people. Also, natural eye contact is not 100% staring into their eyes, no one does that naturally. It's looking at their eyes, but letting your eyes wander a little to their lips, or you looking away while you think of a response. This is more natural. I have a friend who doesn't get this and you can tell he is trying to have eye contact, but instead he just eye fucks you because of the never ending "stare."
