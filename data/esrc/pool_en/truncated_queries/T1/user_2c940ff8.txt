With all the salads now, instead of just stacking ingredients on top of each other, they will be arranged on the perimeter of the salad like a [painter's palette (term used in official materials)](http://static5.depositphotos.com/1027218/433/v/950/depositphotos_4330040-Painters-palette.jpg). The chicken will be diced into 12-16 pieces instead of 4 strips. Bacon bits for the ranch salad are discontinued, although stores will have stock to last quite a while (assuming /u/TehWildMan_ doesn't accidentially drop a cambro full of them). Instead, 3 halfs of bacon will be used, torn into bite-sized chunks on table. This solves a problem where bacon bits were often held at room temperature for days before being used on salads. Table assembly is simple. I am not sure on the exact portion sizes, but our store is using two rail-spoons of the asain veggie blend, and roughly 8 orange slices, along with the diced chicken. EDIT: Forgot that an orange glaze will be applied on the chicken. The stock dressing is a mcdonalds-exclusive packaging based off Newman's Own Sesame Ginger, and sliced toasted almonds are included with the salad.
