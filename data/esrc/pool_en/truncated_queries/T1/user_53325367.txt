FWIW, while this may help one fall asleep, it may also decrease sleep quality, so I wouldn't really recommend it in general because the disadvantage outweighs the benefit for most people. In any case I don't think it's a good idea to rely on any kind of substance as a sleep aid, as you may come to depend on it. Alcohol also relaxes your muscles, including the ones in your mouth, which makes you more prone to snoring, or worse, sleep apnea. Personally, I try to avoid alcohol in the 2-3 hours before going to bed. Mainly because it makes me snore and bothers my spouse. I have no metrics on the quality of my sleep with or without alcohol.
