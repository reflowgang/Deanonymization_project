There are many ways to win a crit, none of which are easy: -Solo away (simply ride faster than the other 39 riders combined) -win the group sprint -make a ballsy solo move with (x) to go (usually one lap to go) -stay out of the wind for the entire race and get lucky somehow Your method should depend on your strengths as a cyclist, which are probably the same as your strengths as a swimmer.
