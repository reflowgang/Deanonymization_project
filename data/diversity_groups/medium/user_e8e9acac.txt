Use Sigurd in chapter one to save all the towns.(You may want another unit to actually visit the town for the gold and the one speed ring) Use the steel sword to dodge all the axes. Towns have 10 "hp", and will lose 1 hp every turn a brigand attacks it. At 10 hp it gives 5000 gold, at 1 it gives 500. Certain towns give out items, sometimes amazing ones. If the town is destroyed, you lose the gold or the item, and many of them have very important story information. Each unit has their own gold, the arena is free, and you can't die.(Units who lose have their HP dropped to 1) So it's an amazing way to gain xp and gold. Units level up to 30, and promote at 20+ in the castle. You will get two pre promotes in the first chapter, including Sigurd, but they are both far below level 20. Edit: You get 7 rounds of progressively stronger enemies, who only differ per chapter for ranged units. You can save at the beginning of each turn, but don't save over the same file as you could put yourself in a losing position on that turn. There are multiple castles you have to capture each chapter, and usually there is a big wave of reinforcements after you capture one. The weapon triangle is a huge deal. Roads increase movement range* but lowers avoid. If an enemy reaches the entrance to your main castle you lose the chapter. Male x Female units can marry, they gain 5 points while standing next to each other for each turn up to turn 50 of each chapter. At 500 points, they marry, but each pairing starts with a certain amount of points. Check your units for the "talk" option for good items and dialogue. When you reach a chapter past the first, look up secret events for that chapter. For example, your axe knight can get a hero axe by going to a certain tile in the second chapter, but you would never know that without looking it up.
