I'd also like to remind people of how the [Modmail](http://www.reddit.com/message/compose?to=%2Fr%2Fgonewildaudio) works. Modmail is basically a shared inbox between moderators of a given Subreddit, meaning that if you send a message to Modmail, *all* of us will get the message and can respond to it. Using the modmail is helpful to us because: 1. It will alert all of us to a potential issue, so we can all be in the loop about it and discuss it. There are some problems too large for one person to make a call on, so it's good to have a perspective from everyone, while avoiding the classic game of "He said, she said." 2. It increases the likelihood of your problem being addressed faster, as at least one of us will see it before the others do. For example, if you're being harassed by another user, you'd probably want that handled ASAP, as it's an immediate problem. To send a modmail, you can click the "message the moderators" link above the list of mods in the sidebar. Alternatively, you can also compose a message and address it to /r/GoneWildAudio (or whatever subreddit you need the mods of), and your message will go to modmail. It's easy! While I personally don't mind receiving mail in my inbox (it's nice hearing from you guys, so say hi sometime!), for more complicated questions or problems, you should all definitely use the Modmail so that everyone can weigh in and reach an appropriate conclusion. I also make it a point to say that things I say in my personal inbox aren't necessarily the opinions of the ModTeam, as you're contacting Lemon the person, not Lemon the Mod. On a related note, if you do choose to **Report** a post for some reason, it's very helpful if you could give us a reason of why you reported it, so we can know why it's offensive at a glance.
