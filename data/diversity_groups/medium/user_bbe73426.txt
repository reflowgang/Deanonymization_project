1) It all depends as to what you want. You may be better served buying a used bike at a LBS. I have found some good deals on bikes for under $200, but I'm also a very competent home mechanic. My wife went from not having ridden a bike for 15 years to daily commuting in DC traffic in roughly a year, but she rode at every possible opportunity. She can now throughly kick my ass through city traffic, thanks to riding in rush hour daily. 3) Take it to a good mechanic. You will learn how to change a tire, make quick adjustments, and perform routine maintenance to keep your bike rolling right and to keep you from getting stranded.
