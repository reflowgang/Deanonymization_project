Something I'd recommend is, if you want to start small, find three other guys (or girls, there are mixed versions) and start up a barbershop quartet! Really freaking good arrangements are easy to come by, with stellar arrangements published by the barbershop harmony society at www.harmonymarketplace.com. Find a local barbershop chorus too.
