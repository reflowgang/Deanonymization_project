Here's a few more tips based on those instructions, just adding what my general experience has been farming MS roulette for Poetics / fleshing out a few details.   * **In Castrum,** when you pull the trash around the pipes, a majority of groups just kill it there before running to 2nd boss. * On 2nd boss, I have rarely seen tanks bother with running to the gate with the boss. * It's important to note that you have to have the trash **in front** of the cannons. A lot of tanks in these groups pull the adds on the search light and don't bring them back, which makes it where the cannons can't hit them. Also, usually a DPS or Healer or even the OT activate the search light while the trash is being killed by cannons. * For the colossi especially, the tank should be ready to stun enemies out of their AoEs to the best of their ability so the cannons don't get nuked. * There's a 2nd set of cannons to pull everything to in this step that is neglected. * "Pulling the boss" isn't super intuitive here. You stand on the remp on the far right end of the room as you enter. When Cid arrives, a few death claws will spawn. After several more moments, the actual boss spawns. MT stays on boss the entire time and ignores all the adds, which usually get AoEd out of existence before they're a problem. * As far as average groups, I have never once see them tank the boss on Cid. * It's worth noting that if the cannons were destroyed in this next part, you have to go back to the 2nd set of cannons instead.  Also, throughout these Castrum trash pull instructions, be **Sprinting,** especially on the last pull. If a Healer didn't come with you on last pull, you will probably need cooldowns to stay alive while you make your way back to the cannons.  * **For Praetorium,** "pull the trash and use the console" is very literal. Unless someone is trying to heal you, when you aggro the trash then touch the console, it stays aggro'd on you even though you're on another floor and won't attack your party members assuming they don't get on the aggro table. You can Flash once as you pull them to cement aggro if you please, but make sure you make it into the console first and foremost. Getting in while you're being hit is obnoxious if you don't get it right the first time. * For the trash after 1st boss, be looking for someone to aggro the Colossus cause someone is almost always straggling and does. * "Getting your drivers license" after the trash pull goes down like this: You open the door like normal, then go in on the right and **everyone touches the console in here,** then gets on the elevator. If you don't touch it, you can't use the Magitek walkers. * At the start of Ultima weapon, a healer will almost always have aggro because their healing numbers aren't being mitigated by Ultima's super armor or whatever. Just wait for his buff to be dispelled then use Provoke to regain aggro.
