This is an interesting matter, if you stay on this sub long enough, you'd be forgiven for thinking that Caffiene (esp. Coffee) is some wonder cure for all aliments and drinking 6 mugs a day is not only *not bad* but is the *ideal minimum*.  As a caffeine addict, like yourself, I call BS on this notion. Whilst I am sure the science into the effects that caffeine has on our physical bodies is solid, there is not doubt that there is an effect on our mental health and our quality of life.  The following thing, in my opinion, negatively affect somebodies quality of life:   * Not being able to think straight in the morning until having had one or many cups of coffee,  * Withdrawal effects (as you've mentioned, when you can't get coffee)  * The cyclic effect it can have on you sleep patterns, (drink coffee, stay up late, need more coffee to get you started the next day).  * The diminishing returns of the 'caffeine kick', I now barely feel the effects of caffiene now, rather it's used to remove the effects of the withdrawl.  A few things I do to cut down the amount of coffee that I drink and reduce the negative effects it has:   * Don't drink coffee past midday, not only does this mean you'll drink less, but it also means that your brain will be used to functioning without it. Finally you are also drinking it when it's going to help you the most and reducing the effects that it'll have on your sleep.  * Drink tea instead of coffee in the afternoon. Still a nice warming drink, has plenty of benefits of it's own too and unlike cocoa is quite low in calories (I add a splash of semiskimmed milk).  * Only drink GOOD coffee. Lifes too short for shit coffee and if there is a bit of a process involved in making it (like a cafetiere, aeropress, esspresso machine) then you might think twice about making it if you're only going to rush it down to fill a gap in the day.
