The term professionals is not defined in the CBA, so does someone playing on loan under an SPC (Pulock in 2014-2015) before the ELC kicks in count as a 'Professional'. It is not defined in the CBA. So really it hinges on whether or not he was a Pro in the year where he played 30 AHL games on loan. So I guess the point I am trying to make is, although by the CBA it would seem like Pulock should be exempt, if the wording on NHL is correct he could still be not exempt. This would however require the league to basically define a professional as anyone who played in the NHL/AHL regardless of contract.
