The policies that result from gerrymandering affect the voting districts, which means those effects roll up towards the national level.

I put one polling location 20 minutes outside Houston to serve that portion of my 'constituents', and another dozen in centrally located spots in all the other little rural towns throughout the district.    Those urban (democratic) voters have to drive out of their way and then wait in long lines to vote, while all of those rural voters are in and out in 5 minutes.
