Adding to the narrative piece, I was contemplating what I could do that would be interactive, and I thought of this:  Go over a brief plot chart to teach the arc of a story. Exposition, conflict, complication, climax, and resolution.  Then you stand in a circle and do One Sentence or One Word story. It's a favorite improv game of mine. Each person can say one sentence or one word (one word is harder) to add to the story. You could give starters for each part, to show students structure. It's like a live action mad lib of sorts.
