Cubers have ~~tenths~~ ~~teens~~ ~~teeth~~ tens, sometimes hundreds of what we call *algorithms* memorized: Pre-defined move sequences intended to take the cube from one state to another but only moving specific pieces towards their desired spots while all the others are kept in place. With muscle memory and quick reflexes, often combined with the ability to "predict" how the cube will turn out after a specific sequence of moves, one is able to solve the cube in a matter of seconds.  That's not to say every solve is pretty much the same - each solve is unique, since there's *43 quintillion* possible permutations for a cube:  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rubik%27s_Cube#cite_note-Vaughen-27  Also, here's the current Top 100 lowest times for solving a 3x3:  https://www.worldcubeassociation.org/results/events.php?eventId=333&amp;amp;regionId=&amp;amp;years=&amp;amp;show=100%2BPersons&amp;amp;single=Single
