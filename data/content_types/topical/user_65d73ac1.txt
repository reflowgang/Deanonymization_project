Before the internet was common, many people used a BBS to communicate.  Speed went from 300 bps to 54,000 bps in about 15 years. That is 40 Bytes per second up to a whopping 6,600 Bytes per second!  Uses included public message boards, private messages, download software and text based, turn based multi-player games.  Many were run by home users, not AOL/GENie/Prodigy.  Some BBSes would dial out to other BBS and forward messages, FidoNet was one of the biggest home-grown systems.   Using a dedicated internet connection at T1 speed (1.5 Mbps!) was a game changer, but the apps stayed the same - message boards, private messages, download software, play games ... kind of like the internet today.

You work in 2014.  File taxes at the beginning of 2015 for 2014.  Get your Notices of Assessment in 2015 for tax year 2015 which gives you your RRSP contribution room based on 2014 income.  You can contribute to your RRSP from Jan 2015 to end of Feb 2016 and claim it on your 2015 taxes.  Then get your next NOA later this year and repeat.  January &amp; February are overlapping months where you can claim the contribution in the current or previous year.    Although you don't have the latest NOA, you can keep contributing monthly in anticipation of the NOA since you know you will have income and thus create RRSP contribution room.
