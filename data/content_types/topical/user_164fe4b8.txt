If by "tank" you mean "build that does lots of being near enemies and soaking up damage" then that's called using an armour build, one of your two general choices when it comes to non-stealth gameplay (the other choice is called dodge).  Because you can unlock multiple skill 'profiles' and switch between them inbetween games, most people have a dedicated non-stealth profile *as well as* a dedicated stealth profile that then then change into and out of as required.  If you are looking to create an armour build, skills such as Iron Man Basic (Enforcer, Tier 6) and Bulletproof basic (Technician, Tier 6) are considered very important due to the *huge* amounts of armour you can have. Perk decks that complement armour builds well include Muscle, Infiltrator and Gambler.  If you're not willing to mod in a custom dozer mask, you could purchase a Dozer Bobblehead mask from the official PD2 merch store and use the bonus Bobblehead mask code you receive with it.
