Here's the deal with almost every complete ever:      The deck will be good. Enjoi and Almost are good brands, professional grade, no problem. This is where most of the quality is in a complete, as this is what people who are looking to buy will see first.      The wheels will be fine as well. Wheels are easy to make, and there isn't much that can go wrong. [Unless you are really unlucky](http://www.reddit.com/r/NewSkaters/comments/39q1ru/one_of_my_bearings_became_extremely_loose_while/).      The bearings and trucks will most likely be of mediocre quality, as these are the parts where it is easiest for the company to cut costs. This is just the way that completes work; it's the reason they are so cheap.      When it all boils down though, the quality is a relative thing, and any name brand complete you get will do fine. I won't preach all the tips that you've probably seen in a hundred videos, but I will say this: it really helps to over exaggerate all of your movements. Take the height you're jumping, the distance you're sliding your foot towards the nose, and double them. Try to jump to the moon, try to slide your front foot all the way off the board.      Over exaggerating your movements will help you realise how much you actually need to move to get the board to perform.
