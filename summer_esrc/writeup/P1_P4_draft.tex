\title{Open-Weight Deanonymization under ESRC:\\
Accuracy, Confidence, and Stage Assignment (P1--P4 Draft)}

\author{DRAFT --- summer\_esrc}
\affiliation{\institution{University of Luxembourg}}

\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
We re-run the Extract--Search--Reason (ESR) deanonymization pipeline with
local open-weight models on Reddit (pool\_en) and Hacker News at truncation
level T8, and compare against archived GPT-4o. Open weights nearly match
GPT-4o on Reddit top-1 accuracy but lag on Hit@15 and fail to transfer to HN,
where long-profile chunking collapses retrieval. Verbalized confidence is
badly calibrated; selected-token logprobs rank better yet still fail as a
reliable 90\%-precision filter. Swapping which stage holds the larger model
does not change accuracy beyond sampling noise, and the calibration failure
reproduces under the swapped stack. Draft for refinement; Gemma-26B Reason
robustness pending.
\end{abstract}
\maketitle

\section{Setup}

We follow the ESRC attack of Lermen et al.: \textbf{Extract} (trait summary
from comments), \textbf{Search} (embedding retrieval of top-$k{=}15$ gallery
candidates with \texttt{all-mpnet-base-v2}), \textbf{Reason} (forced pick among
candidates). Evaluation is at fixed volume \textbf{T8}. Unless noted,
temperature $=0$, thinking disabled, seed $=2026$, bootstrap $n{=}10\,000$.

On Reddit, top-1 is statistically compatible with GPT-4o, but Hit@15 is
significantly worse (Table~\ref{tab:p1}): identical embedder and gallery imply a
\textbf{query-summary} gap from local Extract (often chunk-merged) vs.\ archived
GPT-4o summaries. On HN the gap is large on both metrics. Nearly all HN profiles
are chunked ($497/499$); top-1 falls with chunk count (2--3: 20.2\%; 4--6:
16.6\%; 7--10: 13.4\%; $11{+}$: $0\%$, $n{=}6$). Search collapse (24\% vs.\ 59\%
Hit@15) is the main mechanism---Reason cannot recover IDs never retrieved.

\section{P2--P3 --- Confidence and calibration}

We score Reason picks with (a)~verbalized confidence in $[0,1]$ and
(b)~$\exp(\mathrm{logprob})$ of the selected candidate-id token; plus redesigned
(c$'$): C1 binary confirm $P(\mathrm{YES})$ and C2 mean pairwise win vs.\ top-3
Search rivals.

\textbf{(b) wins ranking / R@P; C1 wins ECE} but does not dethrone (b) for
high-precision selection (Table~\ref{tab:p2}). Isotonic calibration on half-splits
(seed $=42\neq2026$) confirms: naive $\tau{=}0.9$ on (b) is badly overconfident
(delivered $P\ll0.90$). Isotonic approaches $\sim$89\% precision on Reddit (b)
with heavy abstention ($\sim$18\% survivors) but fails within HN and does not
transfer Reddit$\to$HN. HN$\to$Reddit can hit $\sim$97\% precision but only
$\sim$7\% of queries survive. Frame: \textbf{abstention filter}, not an operable
cross-platform 90\%-precision attack.

\section{P4 --- Frontier gap, stage swap, pattern robustness}

\paragraph{C1 (open vs.\ frontier).}
Table~\ref{tab:p1} is the C1 answer: competitive Reddit decisions; clear HN /
Hit@15 deficit with a mechanistic Extract/Search explanation.

\paragraph{C2 (cross-tier).}
Swapping Extract$\leftrightarrow$Reason roles (35b Extract / 4b Reason) yields
point estimates slightly \emph{up} (pool\_en top-1 $36.2{\to}38.7\%$; HN
$16.6{\to}18.2\%$), but all paired $\Delta$ CIs include 0 (e.g.\ top-1
$+2.4$\,pp [$-1.8,+6.8]$; McNemar $p{=}0.33$). Chunking drops sharply (pool\_en
$75\%{\to}18\%$; HN $99.6\%{\to}74\%$) without a significant accuracy gain.
\textbf{Stage assignment is exchangeable within noise} at $n{\approx}500$---we
do \emph{not} support ``the gap concentrates in Reason.''

\paragraph{C3 (calibration under swap).}
Re-scoring cross-tier Reason (\texttt{qwen3.5-4b}) with (a)/(b) reproduces the
same qualitative pattern: verbalized ECE high; (b) better AP; HN (a) R@90\%P
$=0$ on both stacks. Supports a \textbf{task-level} confidence failure, not a
quirk of strong-Reason placement. (\textit{Pending:} Reason$=$\texttt{gemma4-26b}
robustness run.)

\section{Takeaways}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Open-weight ESR nearly matches GPT-4o on Reddit top-1; HN transfer fails
via chunked Extract $\to$ weak Search.
\item Capacity allocation between Extract and Reason is not identifiable at
this scale/pair.
\item High-precision confidence gating remains fragile under open weights
regardless of stage assignment.
\end{enumerate}
